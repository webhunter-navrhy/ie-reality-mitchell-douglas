Description

Mitchell Douglas is delighted to present this unique and beautifully positioned family home, set on a generous private site in one of North Wicklow's most sought after locations.

Nestled behind double entrance gates and surrounded by mature trees, hedging, and landscaped lawns, this charming bungalow offers the perfect blend of privacy, convenience and future potential.

The accommodation comprises of a welcoming living room with feature fireplace, fitted kitchen, three bedrooms and a family bathroom.

The substantial site provided excellent scope to extend or further enhance the property, subject to the necessary planning permission.

Location is everything, and this home delivers ie. - 12 minutes approx from Bray's iconic seafront, 7 mins. approx from the picturesque village of Enniskerry and world renowned Powerscourt Estate and gardens and 500 meters approx.

from St. Gerard's School.

A short drive to the N11/M50, providing easy access to Dublin, city, airport and beyond.

Whether you are seeking a family home, a property with expansion potential, or a tranquil setting within easy reach of excellent schools and amenities, this home offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the very best of Wicklow living. Early viewing is highly recommended.
